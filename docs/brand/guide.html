Spacing

Every gap is a step, never a guess.

One scale for every gap. Things that belong together sit close; groups sit at least twice as far apart. Nothing touches the edge. One alignment. Three levels at most.

Voice

Hopeful, clear, honest, a little defiant.

Ask for a page that tracks every bill. youcoded builds it, right inside the app.
Doone concrete example of what only youcoded does
Pick any AI model: Claude, GPT, Qwen, or run one locally!
Don'theadline what rivals also offer
Build a new look, a new page, a new feature. Then share it with everyone.
Dosell the vision: everyone builds it
Imagine a world where your computer works for you. Sound too good to be true? It isn't.
Don'ttalk down, or stack rhetorical tricks
Unleash a seamless, model-agnostic agentic harness that supercharges your workflow.
Don'tjargon and AI-ad clichés
Coding is obsolete. Developers are about to be replaced.
Don'tdig at people who code

Prefer

  • assistant, app, your own computer
  • any AI model, free, open source
  • pages, themes, add-ons, files
  • asks before it acts

Avoid

  • harness, LLM, inference, agentic
  • model-agnostic, local-first, BYOK
  • artifacts, autonomous
  • "a GUI for Claude Code"

Edit every draft twice

1 · Cut 20% without changing the meaning. 2 · Ask whether every word, frame and page earns its place. Write for smart readers with little time; say each thing once. Nobody should finish a piece asking "so… why not just use Claude Code or Hermes?"

Never write

real app · real files · actually reads · does real work · self-improving · unleash · supercharge · seamless · revolutionize · next-generation · game-changer · your AI copilot
