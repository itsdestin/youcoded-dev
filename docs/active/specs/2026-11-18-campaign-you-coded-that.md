# Campaign: "you coded that."

Written from scratch on 2026-11-18. It deliberately does not draw on any earlier promo film,
script or draft in this workspace (Destin's instruction). Rules come from the brand guide
(`docs/brand/README.md`) and the vision (`docs/vision/VISION.md`).

## The idea

Every app you have ever used, someone else coded. youcoded's whole premise is that everyone who
downloads it becomes the one who makes things, just by asking. The name already says it. So the
campaign never explains; it shows a plain request turning into a real thing, then hands the
viewer the credit: **you coded that.**

The film ends on the paradox that is the product: **you didn't write a single line. you coded
all of it.** Then the words "you" and "coded" close together into the logo.

Why it works: one idea, carried by the name; no feature list; no "imagine a world"; true
(pages, finance trackers, paint apps, trip boards and whole themes are made by asking); the
punchline is the brand. It answers "why not Claude Code?" without saying it: what you see are
finished things for normal life, not code.

## Visual system

- Ground: lavender `#F7F0FB`; dark moments plum-black `#140B1E`. Never pure black or grey.
- App footage floats as a window with a large soft shadow (no device mockups, no drawn UI).
  Real recordings only, practice data only, filmed at 2x+ for sharpness.
- Camera: slow, eased push-ins and glides added in the edit (never shaky, never whip-pans).
  Cuts land on the beat grid.
- Type: DM Sans 900, −5% tracking, tight leading for headlines; Outfit 600 logo; Outfit 600
  web address; DM Sans 500 platforms line. JetBrains Mono not used in films.
- Motion of type: words rise 0.3 of their height with a 400 ms ease-out and a slight blur-in;
  never bounce, never typewriter on supers.
- The gradient (`#9D5BD0` → `#D25AA0`) appears once per piece: the logo.
- Muted-safe: every super holds ≥ 1.2 s + 0.25 s/word.

## Music

Composed in code, 120 BPM (one bar = 2.0 s = 60 frames at 30 fps), mixed to −14 LUFS.
Warm, confident, minimal electronic with organic tone; no chiptune, no EDM drop, no "inspirational
piano". Hero map: bars 1–6 sparse (house), hit + groove from bar 7–8, montage bars 8–19 with
the theme flips on four beats of bar 17–18, breakdown bars 20–23 under the two lines, a hit on
bar 24 for the logo, last chord rings to 60 s.
