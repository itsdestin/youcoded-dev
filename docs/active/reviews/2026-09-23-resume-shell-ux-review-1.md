Static deck preview verified at 1440×900 and 1024×768 in Meadow Mist and Halftone. A fresh visual reviewer confirmed fidelity/legibility at 1440 but found only Today visible at 1024 because the separate notice card forced the comparison into a vertically stacked compact mode. **Triage: accepted and fixed** by dropping the redundant notice and shortening the risk card: a second preview shows Today **and** Matching side by side at 1024, with no answer bar over the crop. The standard deck choice cards still say Option A/B but display their Today/Matching names directly beside the keys; **triage: no engine change** for this scope, since they identify the choice without ambiguity once both pictures are visible. The two-column transcript remains present in the separate real-component views.
