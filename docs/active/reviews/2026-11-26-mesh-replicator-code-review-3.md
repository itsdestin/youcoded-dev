## Findings, most severe first

### 1. CONFIRMED. Calm-then-permanent refusal: the writer's start-up evidence expires after 10 minutes and nothing refreshes it. Ordinary fault: none (normal use).
`replicator.ts:536-545` (`evidenceNow`) accepts a peer's "all heads sent" only while `now - headsComplete <= PEER_FRESH_MS` (10 min). `headsComplete` is set only when a `rep.heads` frame arrives (`replicator.ts:369`). In a quiet, in-sync set nobody sends heads again: the 60-second hello sends a SUMMARY, and equal summaries deliberately skip heads (`onSum`, replicator.ts around line 300). The evidence latch (`run.evidence = true`) is only set when something EVALUATES the gate, i.e. at the first append or status call. So: restart the app, sync, leave it idle for 11 minutes, type a message: refused, and it stays refused.
Repro `$S/t12.test.ts` (two devices, everything healthy, A restarted, user appends after N idle minutes): idle 2 and 9 minutes: `saved`. Idle 11 and 30 minutes: `threw writer-waiting-for-peers`, and still refused 2 minutes later; status `comparing(writer-waiting-for-peers)`.
What the user sees: the chat's new messages stop being saved ("loud" to the adapter, but the status word is the bland `comparing`), with every device online. Needs a restart, or some other device's lines arriving, to clear.
Mutation proof: mutant `ev-fresh` (remove the 10-minute expiry) survives all 228 tests: no test covers an append more than 10 minutes after the start.
Fix direction: latch the evidence the moment complete heads arrive (not when the gate is evaluated), or drop the expiry, or have the writer ask for heads again when it is refused.

### 2. CONFIRMED. QUIET at the moment of "saved", permanent fault afterwards: the evidence gate accepts testimony from ANY single peer, and the "all three restored" limit is the ordinary restore.
Mechanism: `evidenceNow` returns true if any one peer has sent a complete heads list in this process (`replicator.ts:538-540`), whatever that peer holds. Also true after 20 s if no peer was heard and both records are present (`writing-alone`, :543). Also latched by a status read (`statusNow` calls `evidenceNow`, :661, which writes `run.evidence`).
Repros (all: writer A, whole `store`+`replicator`+`witness` restored from an older backup, the peer that holds the newer lines B not yet reachable):
* `$S/t1.test.ts`: (a) an empty/behind third device C answers first: A appends; (b) both peers offline 20 s: A appends. Both: `saved` at position 5 for a different line than the one the adapter was told `saved` for at position 5 before. When B returns: `faulted(writer-signed-two-histories)` on A, B and C, permanently.
* `$S/t7.test.ts`: a hostile paired device M sends an empty "complete" heads list `{page 0 of 1}` or only `{page 1 of 2}` right after A starts: A appends within 2 s, same permanent fault later. Control (no spoof): A refuses `writer-waiting-for-peers`, then refills from B and ends `in-sync:10` on both. So the gate works when the right peer speaks; it cannot tell whose testimony matters.
* Paging: `headsComplete` is set when the LAST page of a list arrives (`replicator.ts:369`), not when all pages did, so lost live frames or a lying sender give "complete" without the page holding this version.
Why it matters: the witness is a sibling folder inside the same `mesh/` directory as `store/` and `replicator/` (`service.ts:318-320`). Any directory-level restore (Time Machine style, copying the profile back) restores all three together, which is the documented invisible case. So the witness only helps against partial restores; for the common restore the only defence is peer testimony, which is weak as above. User-level outcome of "one restore + 20 s offline (or a fresh device chatting first)": messages say saved, later every device shows the chat as faulted and nothing in this folder clears it.
Safer rule: testimony only from every device that is currently online (or all roster devices, with a long named wait), per-page bookkeeping, a minimum wait after start, and put the second record somewhere a profile restore does not reach (OS secret store, or relay-side per-device counter: a restored device's durable counter is behind the relay's, which already answers `duplicate`). `writing-alone` should not apply when the roster has other devices that have been seen recently.

### 3. CONFIRMED. A claim unbacked for 10 minutes is discounted, so the writer can append past a TRUE longer tail held by an honest slow peer. Quiet then permanent fault.
`replicator.ts:597-600`. `$S/t2.test.ts` ("claim discount"): whole restore of A, B holds 10 lines, B's durable frames lost for 15 virtual minutes (relay problem). Append refused (`writer-behind-peer-holds-more`) at 30 s, 5 min, 9 min 20 s, 10 min 20 s; at 15 min A answers `saved` (position 5 reused), status `pulling(peer-claim-discounted ...)`, and after the link heals both A and B are `faulted(writer-signed-two-histories)`. Same family as 2 (needs the restore), but this one needs no hostile or empty peer, only a slow relay. Mutant `claim-discount-always` is killed, so the discount is tested only in the direction "it exists".
Also (hostile, loud): see finding 6.

### 4. CONFIRMED. Calm named stall from a race in `SignedHeads.load` (stale cache), plus a possible wrong "set aside".
`signed-heads.ts:53-72`: two overlapping first loads each read the file and each `set` the cache; a slow first load that read BEFORE `add()` wrote the point overwrites the cache entry that holds it. Unit repro `$S/t6.test.ts`: file has 1 record, cache sees 0. World repro `$S/t4.test.ts`: late device C ends with 10 lines and 1 saved point on disk but `comparing(holds-lines-without-signature)` for the whole run (10+ minutes), it cannot ack above 0 and cannot serve. Self-heals only when the writer next appends. Worse, with a stale cache `endorsedByWriter` and the equivocation check (`add` returning `conflict`) look at the wrong list: a legitimately endorsed copy can be judged "unendorsed" and set aside instead of faulted (reasoned from the code, not run).
Fix: share the in-flight load promise per version; never overwrite an existing cache entry.

### 5. CONFIRMED. One rotted byte in a writer's own record stops that chat for good, and the status is calm for two of the three cases. Ordinary single fault.
The records have no checksum and are only length-checked (`own-record.ts:20`). `$S/t2.test.ts` ("rot of own record"), one bit flipped in one record, then restart:
* high bit of the line count: `writer-behind-own-signed-head` forever, status `refilling(behind-own-signed-head)` (named, but nothing will ever refill it).
* bit in the hash of either record: `writer-own-records-disagree` on every append, status **`in-sync`**.
* low bit of the line count: `writer-diverged-from-own-signed-head` on every append, status **`in-sync`**.
Status only looks at `ev.problem && !ev.effective` (`replicator.ts:658`), so these refusals are invisible there. No recovery path exists in this folder. Also an own record that is rotted-but-the-right-length is trusted as a signing input: `want` re-signs it as `cover` (`replicator.ts:~409`), so a rotted record makes the writer sign a (count, hash) pair it never held (harmless for chains, but not what the doc says).
Fix: CRC on each record; a record that fails its CRC is treated as absent when the other one is valid and consistent; status must show every append refusal reason.

### 6. CONFIRMED (loud, hostile paired device). A paired device can freeze the writer's chat indefinitely; the status mislabels it.
`$S/t8.test.ts`: M claims more lines than the writer holds; discount after 10 minutes, but a claim of FEWER lines resets `since` (`replicator.ts:366`), so M alternates and the writer refuses at minutes 1, 11, 22 and 33. The status flips to `diverged(peer ... holds a different history at the same point)` (it reads M's bogus chain value at line 0), which tells the user their own chat has diverged. An equal-count claim with another chain value (`writer-diverged-from-peer`) has no discount at all. Loud (the adapter gets a named error), but permanent while M stays paired and the doc comment in `appendLocal` says a paired device must not be able to freeze the writer.

### 7. CONFIRMED (caller rule, easy to get wrong). Retry without an expected head duplicates lines, quietly, and the duplicates replicate.
`appendLocal(expected?)` is optional (`replicator.ts:563`). `$S/t3.test.ts` crash sweep over every file operation of an append (4 setups, each K = 1..60 until no crash): with `expected`, every case ended exact-once (`saved` or `already-saved`, 0 duplicates, 0 stuck after one crash, peer up or down). Without `expected`, the 10 `durable-not-recorded` cases and the crash points after the store append (ops 82-86 of that sweep, 15 of 20 outcomes) retried to `saved` and the chat ended with the lines twice on A and on B. Make `expected` required.

### 8. CONFIRMED, low. First-ever append that crashes, peer offline: the writer refuses until a peer returns.
`$S/t3b.test.ts` (crash at each file op of the very first append, peer down at restart): after the crash points that leave only one of the two records, `writer-waiting-for-peers` (named; needs a peer or a roster change). The fallback needs BOTH records. Mutants `ev-alone-haveRecord` and `ev-needs-witness` survive, so no test pins this either way.

### 9. PLAUSIBLE, low. Ack rounds are not durable.
`solicitRound` is in memory (`ctx.ts` VRun), so it restarts at 0 on every process start and after any replicator-folder rollback. A solicited ack that a hostile relay held back across the solicitor's restart would be taken as the answer to the new round with the same number. I could not construct a replay from a paired device (the ack's signature is bound to the sender, I checked), only through the relay holding live frames. Fix: start rounds from a random 32-bit value. Mutants `want-round` and `acks-solicited-fresh` survive.

### 10. CONFIRMED, info. The `bindIds` escape hatch.
`service.ts:321` passes `bindIds: false`; `InterimDirectory`'s own default is `bind:false` (directory.ts constructor); `Replicator.create` defaults to true. Nothing can ship unbound today because the service only starts the replicator in test mode with the flag (`boot-hook.ts`), but the literal `false` has no guard, `repWorld` defaults to unbound so most tests never run the bound path, and mutants `bind-default` and `bind-token` survive. Recommend deleting the option and having the rig derive ids with `deriveRootId`. Residual (documented): any paired device can still create a competing version for any chat id.

### 11. Small, confirmed from reading
* `ownPending` is documented as retried "at the next append and scrub tick"; the scrub tick does not retry it (`replicator.ts:221`). Status stays `stuck(own-record-not-saved)` until the next append.
* `OwnEvidence.replicatorBehind` is computed and never used.
* `evidenceNow` called from `status` mutates the safety latch (a status poll after 20 s of silence latches `writing-alone` for the life of the process, including against a peer that connects a moment later).
* `comparing(copy not set up yet)` is the status for a takeover child that waits for its parent: it names no cause. In fuzz seed 2063 a device sat in it through three checkpoints while the other two said `in-sync`; I could not reproduce it on re-run (timing), so PLAUSIBLE.
* Writer `status` ignores `writer-diverged-from-own-signed-head`, `writer-own-records-disagree` (finding 5).
* `MAX_INCARNATIONS` = 32 retirements then `stuck(reconsider-failed)` forever (idmap.ts); fine unless the disk is bad.
* The off-switch test greps only `store/` and `replicator/` (`off-switch.test.ts:98`), not the new `witness/`; by construction the witness folder is created only on an append, so nothing wrong today.

## Ordinary-user list: single ordinary faults that end in a permanent fault or need a human step
1. Restart, then idle more than 10 minutes, then chat: refused forever (finding 1). No fault needed.
2. Restore the profile from a backup, then chat while the other devices are asleep (20 s) or a fresh device speaks first: `faulted(writer-signed-two-histories)` on every device, permanent (finding 2). Same after a slow relay (finding 3).
3. One flipped bit in one of the writer's own 40-byte records: writer refuses for good, status `in-sync` in two of three cases (finding 5).
4. Lose or restore only the replicator folder on a non-writer: `comparing(holds-lines-without-signature)` until the writer next appends (t4, by design but calm); a third device that needs those lines while the writer is away ends `stuck(no-peer-can-serve)` (named).
5. Delete the witness folder (or lose one record) with the other device gone for good: `writer-waiting-for-peers` until the dead device is removed from the roster.
6. A hostile or buggy paired device claiming more/another chain: writer frozen (finding 6), needs unpairing.
7. Crash during the first append of a chat with the peer away: refused until the peer is back (finding 8).
8. After a single-bit rot of a record where the right-length garbage looks like a record: no recovery without deleting the files by hand.
