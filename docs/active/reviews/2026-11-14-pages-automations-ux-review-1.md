## Summary

I could do every part of the task except finding the daily-limit state, which never appeared by clicking or with `autoView=limit` (U1). The most confusing moment was after pressing "Delete automation": nothing seemed to happen because the confirmation bar sat below the visible area of the dialog (U2). Permission menus that run off the bottom of the dialog (U4) came a close second.
