**Two ways of showing Plan settings — I preferred the inline row (the `planSettings=inline` version).** With the gear-icon popup, a modal window drops on top of the very plan card you're configuring, so you lose sight of the plan while you're adjusting its settings — you have to close the popup to compare your changes against the steps again. The inline version expands underneath the plan, in the same card, so the steps, the cost estimate, and the settings are all visible together the whole time, and I could still see the "Approve" button without closing anything first. The inline version did make the card taller, but even the 15-specialist "heavy" plan's settings only had 3 step-model rows (one per top-level step, not one per specialist), so it never got unreasonably long. The only downside I saw is that on a very long chat, the settings panel pushes the next messages further down while it's open — a small trade worth it for not losing context.
