# Search pages: one pitch per app people are leaving

Destin's direction (deck round 2): people search broad, vague words ("open source ChatGPT
alternative", "easy open source AI assistant"); each page serves one real audience, tuned to
what that app does well and badly; the framing is the "Yours." campaign's, never "make your own
app". Revised after a fresh review (2026-11-27): pitches had drifted into feature lists, repeated
each other, promised the Mesh early and overstated privacy.

## The shared spine (written once, used on every page)

One "Yours." paragraph, the campaign's own words: everything became a subscription; your home,
your money, your look, your rules; stop renting your life; just ask for it, and it's yours. Each
page then earns its OWN first screen against its rival; the spine sits lower and is identical, so
the pages never restate it in new words. Every page carries the non-affiliation sentence.

**Privacy, said honestly everywhere:** YouCoded itself never receives what you send a model; a
cloud model's company (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) still does. Truly private = a free model on your
own computer. Never "your data stays private" with a cloud model.

## 0. The homepage (the broad searches)

Visible text never says "open source AI assistant" in a sentence. Add one subtitle line and one
short paragraph: "an open source alternative to ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini", linking to the pages
below. Catches "open source AI assistant", "easy open source AI assistant", "free AI assistant".
No new design; wording edited by Destin on the real page.

## 1. Leaving the Claude app

- **Who / searches:** people who like Claude and want control. "open source Claude alternative",
  "Claude desktop Linux".
- **Concede (specific):** Anthropic's models, connectors, polish; since 2026-11-24 artifacts can be
  installed as apps on Mac and Windows (support.claude.com/en/articles/18516354, beta).
- **First screen:** your Claude plan, your app, your rules. Keep the model you like; the app around
  it is yours: its look and its pages change to fit you, on Linux and Android as well as Mac and
  Windows. One closing line: any other model, or a free offline one; open source.
- **Honest privacy:** with Claude, Anthropic still sees your messages; offline models are the private option.
- **Proof:** a real clip: sign in with a Claude plan, restyle the app, add a page, in two sentences.
- **Check first:** what Claude's installed apps can change (test before contrasting); cite platforms with date.

## 2. Leaving ChatGPT (the spine's home page)

- **Who / searches:** the biggest, least technical audience. "open source ChatGPT alternative",
  "free ChatGPT alternative".
- **Concede:** best-known assistant, voice, images, app directory.
- **First screen:** the campaign opening, almost word for word: sign in, subscribe, renew, upgrade to
  continue. Then: free; bring your ChatGPT plan if you have one; then it becomes yours.
- **Proof:** a real clip of one everyday ask turning into something kept (not the campaign film).
- **Check first:** price tiers and the ads claim, with official dated sources, or cut them. No hint of
  importing old ChatGPT chats unless that exists.

## 3. Leaving Google (Gemini)

- **Who / searches:** people pulling away from Google, mostly on Android. "Gemini alternative",
  "de-Google AI assistant".
- **Concede:** free tier, Gmail/Docs/Android integration, Opal mini-apps.
- **First screen:** Destin's story, quoted from his voice notes: he left Google, Microsoft and Apple.
  The real hook: Google keeps chats a reviewer has read for up to three years, even after you delete
  your activity (support.google.com 13594961, dated). Then: the assistant runs on your Android phone
  itself, and a free offline model on your computer means nobody reviews anything.
- **Cut:** the Mesh (until released), Office as the lead, "an account you control" (sync is GitHub,
  owned by Microsoft; say so if mentioned).

## 4. Leaving Hermes Agent

- **Who / searches:** people who chose open source and own their data. "Hermes agent GUI", "easy Hermes".
- **Concede:** free and open, memory that learns, scheduled tasks, chat-app reach, a desktop app on Mac and Windows.
- **First screen:** an app you can hand to anyone in your house: no terminal, no setup, one installer on
  every system including Linux and Android. Then what an agent in a chat app can't be: the place itself,
  with its look and pages shaped to you.
- **Check first:** Hermes desktop on Linux (unverified); our own scheduled tasks ("check first").

## 5. Leaving OpenClaw

- **Who / searches:** "OpenClaw without terminal", "OpenClaw alternative", "Hermes vs OpenClaw".
- **Concede:** no-terminal desktop app, WhatsApp/iMessage reach, big skills catalog.
- **First screen:** add-ons you can trust: every skill and theme in the YouCoded marketplace is
  open to read before you install (verify the review process before claiming more), and the app asks
  before acting, with every standing permission on one screen. Then the place itself, shaped to you.
- **Never claim:** a big catalog (13 made for YouCoded, 2026-11-18). ClawHub incident only with an official source.
