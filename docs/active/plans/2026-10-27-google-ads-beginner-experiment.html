The promo is almost certainly “spend $300, get $300,” not free money up front. You pay the first $300 yourself; the credit arrives up to 35 days after you hit the threshold, then expires 60 days later. It is not a discount, and it cannot cover the spend that earns it.

First: it’s Google Ads, not AdSense

Easy to mix up, and they’re opposites. AdSense is for publishers — you put ads on your site and get paid. Google Ads is where you buy the ads. The free-ad-spend promo belongs to Google Ads.

The catch: the offer only exists for accounts less than 14 days old when you redeem it, and it’s applied at signup when you enter billing. So the order matters — do not create the account until you’re ready to spend, or you’ll burn the eligibility window.

The experiment design

You asked for a small budget spread across many categories. That’s the right instinct for learning and the wrong setup for optimization — so the design separates the two jobs.

  • One campaign, six ad groups. One category each, so every category gets its own keyword list, its own ad copy, and its own tracking link.
  • All six live from day one. ~$1.67/day each. Enough to gather a signal, not enough to waste much on a dud.
  • One decision per week. Cut the two worst categories, push their budget to the best performer. By week three you’re concentrated on what works.
  • Maximize Clicks bidding, no conversion tracking. You have no Google tag, so Smart Bidding can’t run. Manual-style bidding is also the correct call for a beginner — you see exactly what each click costs.

Why your own dashboard is the scoreboard. YouCoded already has campaign short links (youcoded.ai/?k7qm2x) and already records visits, installer clicks and downloads per campaign. That means you can judge this experiment without installing a Google tag on the site — and without breaking the site’s privacy promise.

Do it in this order

  1. Set up the six tracking links first. In the analytics dashboard, create one campaign per category — google / brand, google / opensource, google / local, google / frontend, google / phone. Copy each short link. Do this before spending a cent, so week one isn't invisible.
  2. Open the offer page, then sign up. Use Google's new-advertiser offer so the credit attaches. Add the payment method during signup — that's a condition of the offer.
  3. Skip the guided setup. Google will push Smart campaigns and Performance Max. Choose "Switch to Expert Mode" and build a plain Search campaign.
  4. Build the campaign with the six ad groups above — correct final URL per group from step 1, all negatives at campaign level, settings as tabled.
  5. Wait for the first 100 clicks before changing anything. Early edits restart the learning and teach you nothing.
  6. Weekly review, one decision. Cut the two worst categories by downloads-per-visit, move their budget to the best.

How you'll know it worked

Cost per download< $15$15–30> $30

Your benchmark is your own site, not an industry average. Over 43 days, search-comparison traffic converted at 33–48% while Reddit converted at 2%. If ad traffic lands near 40%, paid search behaves like search and is worth scaling. If it lands near 2%, ad clicks aren't search intent — and that's the finding, not a failure.
