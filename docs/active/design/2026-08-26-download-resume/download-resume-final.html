Why / details

What did not change: it is still a solid fill with black text. That is the part carrying the contrast, and softening it back into a translucent tint is precisely what made the label invisible on Crème and Light two rounds ago.

So I shrank it rather than quietening its colour, and re-measured at the smaller size: 6.65:1 on Crème, against the 4.5 needed for 9px text. It is smaller and still readable, which is the only version of "less prominent" worth having.
