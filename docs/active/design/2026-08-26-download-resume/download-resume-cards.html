Why / details

The text is doing a real job — it tells you the download isn't a dead end, and that re-downloading will pick up where it stopped. The question is only whether it should shout. Arguments both ways:

  • Keep it always visible: the situation is rare and confusing, and a user who sees "Unfinished" with only a Delete button has no idea what to do. Hiding the answer behind an icon is how people end up deleting 74 GB they didn't have to.

C The confirmations · Pressing Discard, and pressing Delete8 / 9

Two different words for removing things: unfinished downloads are "Discard", finished models are "Delete".

→ Use "Delete" everywhere, so there's one word for one idea.

judgment
Why / details

The case for two words: they really are different acts. Deleting a finished model throws away something that works; discarding an unfinished download throws away something that never worked. The case for one: a person scanning a settings list shouldn't have to learn a vocabulary to remove a file.

I lean toward keeping both words, because the confirmation text differs anyway ("removes the model file" vs "removes every downloaded piece"), and the two buttons sit right next to each other in the same list where the distinction is visible.
