P-3 Themes dialog

Settings → Appearance
  1. 1
    The active card grows a second line and its neighbour stretches to match — an empty strip with a floating pencil (Dark 65 px vs Crème 34 px).measured
    → Put “active” on the name line, so every card is one height.
  2. 2
    The pencil on a built-in opens a 588 px dialog that holds one sentence (“Built-in themes are locked…”).measured
    → Grey out the pencil on the four built-ins, with a tooltip saying why; community themes keep a live pencil (they have glass sliders).
    why

    All four built-ins are flat-colour themes, so the editor has no sliders to show — only the locked notice. Alternative: hide the pencil entirely; I recommend against it because the tooltip is the only place the why gets explained.

  3. 3
    “Browse all themes →” and “Browse Theme Marketplace” sound like the same thing; they open different screens (your installed themes vs the Marketplace).measured
    → Rename them “All installed themes →” and “Get more themes”.
    why

    The ledger claimed both open the same screen — wrong on checking: the first opens Your Library › Themes (the grid above only shows starred themes), the second the Marketplace.

  4. 4
    Three stacked full-width buttons in three styles; the first is a hand-rolled fourth style the shared Button does not have.judgment
    → The first becomes a quiet text-link row under the grid, leaving one primary (Build) and one secondary (Get more themes).
