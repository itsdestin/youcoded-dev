P-15 — Every dialog gets a title and a ✕ — and no Cancel beside it measured

The problem

Report a bug and Donate were the only dialogs with no title row and no ✕ — Escape was the only way out. The Development menu that leads to the bug report drew its own small uppercase heading instead of the shared header. In the Close session prompt, the footer was the tallest row of the dialog: "Don't show again" wrapped onto two lines beside its toggle and the primary button broke into "Close / session".

(The ledger also said About and Permissions cut their content with no scroll cue — checking the code, both already have the fade. Nothing to do there; the claim was stale.)

What changed (as revised)

  • Report a bug, Donate and the Development menu pass a title to the shared Dialog, which draws the same header every other dialog has (title · ✕). Bug report's title follows the Bug/Feature switch.
  • Donate is retitled Support YouCoded; with a ✕ to dismiss it, the Cancel button is gone and the body is one line of explanation and one full-width action, Open Buy Me a Coffee.
  • Close session: the header is the shared one (title + session name); Cancel is gone (the ✕ is cancel); "Don't show again" stays bottom-left on the standard switch, and with a single button on the right nothing wraps.

What you will notice, and the risks

  • Three dialogs gain a title row and a ✕. Two lose their Cancel button — the ✕ (and Escape) do that job. Risk: someone used to clicking Cancel in the close-session prompt reaches for the ✕ instead; nothing else changes.

P-18 — Tool cards: a real divider instead of a typed "|"; AskUserQuestion looks like its siblings mixed

The problem

Measured: every tool-card header, and the button row of the full-auto stop card, used a typed | character as a divider. It takes the text colour rather than the theme's line colour, sits on the text baseline instead of centred, and screen readers read it aloud. It appears on every tool call in every conversation.

Judgment: the AskUserQuestion card's header stopped after its label (ⓘ | Framework ▾) while every other card reads label ↳ detail; its option rows used their own corner radius and colours; and its radio/checkbox glyph's rounding was written in a way the styling tool cannot see, so it may not have been drawn round at all.

What changed

  • Measured: both dividers are now a 1-pixel line element in the theme's edge colour, centred, invisible to screen readers.
  • Judgment: the AskUserQuestion header now reads Framework ↳ Which framework should the new dashboard use? like its siblings; its option rows use one shared row style (medium corners, edge-coloured border, accent tint when selected); the round/square glyph is now written so it actually renders.

What you will notice, and the risks

  • The pipe character is gone from every tool card; a thin line sits in its place.
  • AskUserQuestion's header carries the first question, so you can read what is being asked without expanding; its rows look like the rest of the app's rows. Risk: this is the card you interact with most, so its shape changing is the most noticeable thing in Phase B — that is why it is tagged judgment.
