## What to produce

Only inside your own folder `vision-options/<your-name>/`. Touch nothing else in the repo; do not
commit.

1. `index.html` — one self-contained page (inline CSS/JS, no network), generated by a script
   `build.py` in your folder that reads the real data files above. Must work opened by file path.
2. `shot.png` — 1440x900 screenshot of the page as first opened, plus `shot-detail.png` showing the

3. `NOTES.md`, under 300 words: the idea in two sentences; how it answers (a) which major systems are
