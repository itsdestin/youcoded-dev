A free local model and a paid model whose price is not published produce a pixel-identical bar — I compared the two screenshots and the row is byte-for-byte the same. Both simply hide the cost chip.

→ When the model is metered but has no published price, show a dimmed "Cost: not listed" chip instead of hiding it.

measured

Risk: "Not listed" is a fourth thing to explain; some users will read it as a bug.

Why / details

Flip between the two pictures. "Paid model" shows Cost: $1.37. "No listed price" shows nothing — and so does a free local session. The three states collapse into two on screen.

That matters because one of them is spending real money. Someone on a metered provider whose model has no price entry sees exactly what a free local user sees, and can reasonably conclude the session is free. Today the only place that distinction exists is the Customize menu, several clicks away.

My recommendation is Yes. This is the one gap in the whole design that can cost the user money.

Why / details
