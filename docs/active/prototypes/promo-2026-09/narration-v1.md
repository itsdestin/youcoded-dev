# The host's lines — narration v1

The host is a fun little narrator walking us through the app. Every line is said in a
speech bubble beside its head at the moment the thing happens on screen, and is gone
before the next one. Lines are short so the bubble stays beside the head (about seven
words is the ceiling). The headline under the window stays as it is.
