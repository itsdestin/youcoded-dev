What each engine heard

EngineTextTook× realtimeWord errors

Took = time after you stopped, for the whole recording. × realtime = took ÷ recording length (0.10 means a 10 s message takes 1 s). Word errors = % of the sample's words missed, wrong or extra; under 5 % reads clean, 5–10 % needs a fix or two, over 15 % is unusable.

Two ways to show words while you talk

Left: an engine built to hear audio as it arrives — words land almost instantly, never rewrite, but it has no punctuation of its own, so a small punctuation model adds it. Right: a whole-message engine re-hears everything since the last pause about once a second; the last words (grey) may shuffle until the next pass. This is what Whisper-style engines must do to look live.
