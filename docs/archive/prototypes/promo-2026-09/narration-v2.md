# The host's lines — narration v2

**What changed from v1, and why.** Every line is now written to the shot it sits on. The music
cuts every 2.1 seconds (one bar), and your reading rule is 1.2 s plus a quarter second a word,
so a shot that lasts one bar fits **two words**, two bars fit about six, and the film's
timing code now *refuses* a line that does not fit (it used to push the line later without
telling anyone — that is how "Ooh. Golden hour." landed on Strawberry Kitty, and how
"There's your brief", "See? Any of them" and "All synced" were pushed out of their sections
and never showed at all).

The voice: a small, dry, cheerful presenter. It says the thing on screen in the fewest words,
never explains, never sells. No line repeats the headline under the window.

Every line also names the thing it is ABOUT, and the host stands beside that thing and
points at it (the arm is aimed at the real spot, the eyes follow, the bubble goes on the far
side so it never covers what he is pointing at). "Stays" means he speaks from where he is.

Edit any line in the right-hand column in your own words. Word counts matter: the
**Fits** column is the most words that slot can hold.

## Things I'd flag

- **"Watch this."** is the weakest line: it is a place-holder for the 2.1 s the typed request is
  on screen before the first flip. The headline already says "Describe a look." An alternative
  is no line there at all — he points at the request and the first bubble is "Golden hour."
- **"Claude, cloud, or local."** stands in for the v1 idea "any model" — the three options the app
  offers. If you would rather name what is on screen, the list shows Claude, GPT, Grok and a
  local Qwen: "Four favourites. Pick one." fits.
- **"Grok it is."** is the only line that is a joke about the choice rather than the feature.
- **Every one-bar slot is two words.** If you want longer lines on the theme flips or the
  take-over prompt, the fix is more bars for those shots (slower music or a longer section),
  not longer text.
