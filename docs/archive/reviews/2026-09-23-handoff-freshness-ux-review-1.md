Conclusion: I can choose to wait, retry, or open the saved copy, but cannot verify a complete handoff here: this prototype intentionally does not simulate transfer or confirmed receipt. The most confusing moment is deciding whether “Open saved copy” is safe when recent messages may still exist only on the other computer. Clicking “Try again” returned to waiting; “Open saved copy” and “Leave it” closed the prompt, with no real conversation destination simulated. At 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844 the actions remained visible; the clipped preview is the visible layout concern. The stress URL was checked, but its visible transcript was effectively fixed (two instances of the same answer in both normal and stress page text), so a 2,000-row performance or overflow claim is not possible. Touch input and high-density display were not tested.
