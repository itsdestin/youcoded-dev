## Triage (implementing session, 2026-10-22)

- U1 accepted — practice data only: the fake notes were 1.3.0's under a 1.3.2 → 1.4.0 update. The ready states now carry 1.4.0's notes. (About's "1.3.0" is the practice app's own version, not part of this feature.)
- U2 accepted — the line under the title now follows the switch: off reads "Downloaded. Restart when you are ready."
- U3 rejected — where the switch lives is Destin's answer (Q-switch: beside Get beta builds, in both places). The group name it lands in goes on the review deck as a question.
- U4 accepted in part — the switch's two descriptions now say the same thing at two lengths ("Downloads new versions by itself"). The beta row's wording predates this feature and is out of scope.
- U5 rejected — the close-window prompt's words are Destin's own, word for word (2026-09-24), and he asked for "the standard closeout prompt" here.
- U6 rejected — Resume starting off is Destin's answer (Q-default, 2026-09-24); ✕ and Escape are the guide's way out of every popup.
- U7 rejected — same as U5.
- U8 rejected — the notes card's padding is unchanged by this feature (the shared card recipe).
- U9 rejected — "Session Naming · Workbench" is the practice app's own label, which only development copies show.
- U10 accepted — the switch no longer says "when you quit", which is false on computers that need a password; the line under the title says when this computer installs it.
- U11 rejected — the real app closes within a second of Restart; the practice app parks there because a browser tab cannot restart.
