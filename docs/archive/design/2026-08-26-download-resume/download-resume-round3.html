Why / details

Flip to Before on the Crème tab: DOWNLOAD INTERRUPTED is rendered, just not visible. On Midnight the same code measured 5.62:1, which is exactly why it looked fine in the screenshots I sent you — I was checking the theme where it happened to work.

It took two goes, and the first was wrong for an instructive reason. My first fix used white text on amber-700, which in stock Tailwind is a dark brown (#B45309) and would have scored 5.02:1. But this app remaps that name: globals.css:294 sets --color-amber-700 to a bright orange (#FF9800), where white scores 2.06:1 — still failing. I only caught it because I re-sampled the pixels after shipping the fix instead of assuming it had worked.

The version you are looking at uses the app's three constant status colours with black text, which measures 9.7 / 7.6 / 5.0. Black rather than white because white fails on two of the three.

Two things worth keeping: the screenshot rig's contrast report never sampled this element, so a clean contrast report is not proof of contrast. And a translucent strip cannot be made safe across six themes at all — it takes its final colour from whatever sits behind it. A solid strip can.
