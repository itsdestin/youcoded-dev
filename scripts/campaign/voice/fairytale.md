# Take 1, "Once upon a time": the narration

Words in [square brackets] are acting cues for the narrator: performed, never spoken.
Everything under the line is what gets recorded. Source of the words: `docs/active/design/2026-11-19-campaign-round2/takes.md`.

---

[mischievous, fast-paced, energetic storybook narrator] For more than a decade, a small handful of corporations have dictated how you interact with your technology.

[dry wit, quick] They decided what your computers could do, what it would cost, and what happened with your personal data.

[conspiratorial, quick] And we all... we all just accepted it. We accepted that we would subscribe to everything and own nothing, and that our privacy would be the cost of convenience.

[quick whisper] However, recently... [bright, rising wonder] something truly wonderful happened.

[with relish] Blinded by their own hubris, these corporations created a new kind of technology: artificial intelligence. A technology they believed would bring about a great treasure: [dryly] shareholder value.

[gathering speed, delighted] But in their excitement, they created something far beyond their own control: a tool that can make software, the ever-scarce foundation of these monopolies, for anyone who asks.

[warm, proud, brisk] And so, for the first time in a very long while, we get to decide. What our computers can do. What it costs. And what happens with our data.

[rousing] It's time to free ourselves from the tech monopolies of the past.

[triumphant] It's time, at long, long last... to open source everything.
