# Narration: "Yours." (the long film; length set by the narration)

Edit the words below freely. Words in [square brackets] are acting cues for the narrator. They
are performed, not spoken, e.g. [whispers], [excited], [slowly], [short pause]. A blank line is
a longer pause. Everything under the line is what gets recorded.

---

[energetic, mischievous, brisk] For more than a decade, a small handful of corporations have dictated how you interact with your technology.
[quick, dry wit] These software monopolies decided what your computers could do, what it would cost, and what happened with your personal data.
[lively, conspiratorial] And we all... we all just accepted it. We accepted that our data was not ours, that we would subscribe to everything and own nothing, and we contorted ourselves to work within the boundaries those corporations created for us.

[quick whisper] However, recently... [bright, rising wonder] something truly wonderful happened.
[with relish, fast] Blinded by their own hubris, these corporations created a new kind of technology: Artificial General Intelligence. A technology they believed would allow them to capitalize in new markets and maximize shareholder value, rarely stopping to consider the consequences for those who might be harmed.
[punchy, gathering speed] But in their blindness, in their excitement, they created something far beyond their own control: the ultimate tool to free ourselves from those very monopolies. A technology that can build software, the ever-scarce foundation of these monopolies, faster than any single corporation.

[thrilled] So imagine a world where your technology finally answers to you.
Where anyone, anywhere, can build exactly what they need... simply by asking for it.
[rapid, excited] A home that runs the way you live. A budget that keeps your secrets. A game for a niece, a tool for a tiny shop, a computer that, at last, feels like your own.
[building fast] And imagine that whatever anyone makes, they can share... so everyone else can use it, change it, and make it better.
[proud, energetic] Software built not by a corporation, but by all of us. Running on our own devices. Holding our own data. Answering to no one but the people who use it.

[rousing, powerful] It's time to free ourselves from the tech monopolies of the past.
[triumphant] It's time, at last... to open source everything.
